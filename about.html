About Me

Hi, I'm Neil Maushard! I am a mechanical engineering student passionate about bringing mechanical systems to life through hands-on custom hardware, actuation, and embedded programming. Working at the intersection of mechanical design, sensor integration, and control systems is what drives my engineering interests.

Beyond CAD models and microcontrollers, I have many non-academic passions such as exploring the outdoors, playing heated board games with friends, traveling with my wonderful girlfriend, trying new foods, and cooking unique meals. While I love building robots, it's these experiences that have made me who I am and help me step back and appreciate the world around me.
