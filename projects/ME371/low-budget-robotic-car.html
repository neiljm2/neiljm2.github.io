Low-Budget Gear-Shifting Robotic Car

Skills Used

Gearbox Design Mechanical Transmission CAD Modeling Rapid Prototyping Embedded Systems SolidWorks

Project Overview

  • Course & Context: Designed as a functional mechatronics build for UIUC's ME 371 (Computer Control of Mechanical Systems) course.
  • In-House Design: Created the multi-ratio gearbox layout, spur gear profiles, and shift mechanical linkages from scratch.
  • My Role: Led the SolidWorks CAD modeling, gear tolerance math, and rapid-prototyping assembly.
  • Objectives: Designing a shift gearbox capable of swapping speed and torque on-the-fly under motor load.

Interactive 3D CAD Assembly of George Mobile (Click and drag to rotate, scroll to zoom).

CAD & Mechanical Linkages

The car was modeled in CAD, optimizing the gear tooth profiles and chassis mounting plates for durability. We utilized 3D-printed PLA gears with custom tooth spacing to avoid binding and minimize transmission friction under motor load. The car layout was developed in SolidWorks, validating linkages and gear ratios.

Outcome

The robotic car successfully executed shift commands, swapping gear ratios dynamically while under drive load. The 3D-printed transmission survived high-torque tests without shearing teeth, proving the efficacy of budget rapid-prototyping materials for functional gearboxes.
