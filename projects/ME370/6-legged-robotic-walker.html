6-Legged Robotic Walker

Skills Used

Linkage Synthesis Kinematics Mechanical Eng. Chassis Design Dynamic Loading SolidWorks

Project Overview

  • Course & Context: Synthesized as a mechanical design prototype for UIUC's ME 370 (Mechanical Design I) course.
  • In-House Design: Modeled the four-bar linkages, tripod gait sync, and chassis plates.
  • My Role: Led the linkage synthesis math, SolidWorks modeling, and structural stress analysis.
  • Objectives: Achieving a stable hexapod tripod gait with optimal ground clearance and minimal joint play.

Interactive 3D CAD Assembly of Legged Walker (Click and drag to rotate, scroll to zoom).

Linkage Synthesis & Structural CAD

The walking legs were synthesized to achieve optimal ground clearance and stride length. The linkages were modeled in CAD, analyzing joint forces and bending stresses under dynamic loading. Custom pins and bearings were selected to minimize mechanical play in the joints.

Outcome

The robotic walker successfully coordinated its leg phases to walk in a stable tripod gait. The four-bar linkage design maintained contact with the ground throughout the stride, proving the structural durability of the synthesized linkages.
