Project Overview

  • Course & Context: Developed for UIUC's SE 423 (Mechatronics) course as an autonomous robotics project.
  • In-House Design: Designed the sensor fusion matrix, motor drive mounts, and LabVIEW control layout.
  • My Role: Focused on control loop coding, sensor calibration, and LabVIEW dynamic mapping.
  • Objectives: Navigating target spaces autonomously via SLAM, localization, and launching a golf ball into a cup.

Figure 1: SLAM Golf Putter team assembly and system testing setup.

Mechanical & Sensor Integration

The chassis was constructed using custom mounts to align the motor shafts and sensor arrays. We integrated high-resolution wheel encoders for odometer readings alongside distance sensors to feed localized spatial matrices into the mapping controller. This sensor fusion loop ensured drift correction during active navigation.

Control Loop Design

The low-level controls were written in C, handling PID velocity loops for the drive motors. LabVIEW was utilized to build the high-level supervisory controls and path planning interface, allowing real-time trajectory visualization and parameter tuning during autonomous putting runs.

Outcome

The autonomous putter successfully completed its spatial mapping routine and completed putting trials with high repeatability. The control software reliably calculated the necessary launch velocity vector, correcting for odometer slip and drift in real-time.
